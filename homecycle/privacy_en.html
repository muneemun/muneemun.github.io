HomeCycle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · Operator: Muni Studio

Muni Studio ("we", "us") respects the privacy of users of the HomeCycle mobile app (the "App") and complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, and the Google Play Developer Program Policies. This policy explains what information the App handles and the choices you have.

1. Our approach

  • The App has no sign-up or login. We do not collect personally identifying information such as your name, phone number or email address.
  • We do not operate servers that store your data. What you enter in the App is stored only on your device.
  • However, the Google advertising, billing and text recognition components built into the App send the information described in Section 3 to Google.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following information stays in your device's app storage and is never sent to us.

  • Item data: names of groceries and supplies, storage location, purchase/expiry/replacement dates and cycles, quantity, notes and purchase price
  • Usage records: items marked as eaten, tossed or replaced, and the monthly summary calculated from them
  • Settings: app language, notification on/off and time, whether you purchased Premium, and when your ad reward (extra slots) expires
  • Summary data shown in the home screen widget (names and counts of upcoming items)

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may back up this data to your own Google account. That backup is handled by Google according to your device settings, and we cannot access it.

3. Information sent off the device and third-party services

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties at our discretion. The App uses the following Google services, which process the information described below.

  • Google AdMob (ads): to show ads and measure their performance, your advertising ID, device information (model, OS version, language, etc.), approximate location based on IP address and ad interaction data are sent to Google. Ads appear as an ad card within lists and as rewarded ads that you choose to watch. If you purchase Premium, the App no longer loads ads.
  • Google ML Kit (photo and receipt text recognition): text in your photos is recognized on the device; photos and recognized text are not sent off the device. To improve the feature and diagnose errors, usage statistics such as device information, app information, a per-installation identifier and processing performance are sent to Google.
  • Google Play Billing (Premium purchase): payments are processed by Google Play and payment details such as card numbers are never shared with us. The App only checks whether you purchased and stores that on your device.

Processing by these services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), How Google uses information from ad partners (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and the ML Kit Terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms). This information may be transferred to Google servers outside your country and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The App does not include any separate analytics or crash reporting tools.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera (optional): to photograph receipts and packaging to recognize items and dates
  • Saved photos: the app does not request photo access. It reads only the photos you pick yourself in the system photo picker, and recognizes them on the device.
  • Notifications (optional): to send expiry and replacement reminders
  • Run at startup: to keep reminders scheduled after a restart (reminders may arrive a little after the set time, depending on the device's power saving)
  • Internet: to show ads, verify purchases and send ML Kit usage statistics

If you do not grant an optional permission, you can still use the rest of the App.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Items and usage records on your device are kept until you delete them.
  • Settings › Clear All Data immediately deletes all registered items and usage records. This cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all App data stored on your device.
  • We keep no user data on servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view, edit or delete items, or clear all data, in the App at any time.
  • Opting out of personalized ads: on Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. (Menu names may vary by device.)
  • You can turn notification and camera permissions on or off at any time in your device settings.

7. Children

The App is intended for users aged 14 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

8. Security

App data is kept in app-private storage that Android isolates per app, and data sent off the device travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
